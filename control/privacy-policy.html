Privacy notice

Effective 5 August 2026

Control is not currently enabled to connect to live QuickBooks data. This notice describes the intended owner-only pilot and the safeguards that must be verified before activation.

1. Operator and controller

Control is operated by Barton Solutions Limited (the “Operator”). Its contact address for this service is Unit R28, Ragged Staff Wharf, Gibraltar. Barton Solutions Limited determines the purposes of the business-record processing described in this notice. Questions and rights requests can be sent to info@era.food.

2. What Control does

Control is a private, owner-authorized connection between one or more QuickBooks Online companies and a local Model Context Protocol server used through OpenAI Codex. Its intended initial purpose is to retrieve selected accounting reports and supporting records so the owner can review performance, reconcile sources and identify exceptions.

The initial pilot disables create, update and delete tools inside the local application. Intuit’s OAuth authorization uses the broad QuickBooks accounting scope; the read-only boundary is therefore an application control rather than a separate permission offered by Intuit.

3. Data, people and sources

Depending on the owner’s specific request, Control may retrieve:

  • company identity, accounting settings and chart-of-accounts data;
  • balances, general-ledger entries and management reports;
  • aged receivables, aged payables and vendor-expense information;
  • transaction references, dates, amounts, payment status and narratives;
  • names and business contact details for customers, suppliers, workers, directors, contractors or other people appearing incidentally in accounting records.

Data comes from the QuickBooks company explicitly selected by its administrator, the owner’s instructions, and any supporting records the owner deliberately provides. The pilot does not intentionally request payroll files, full employee records, attachments or payment-card credentials, but limited personal information may appear incidentally in ledgers and reports.

4. Purposes and lawful bases

Data is used only to answer the owner’s accounting and reporting instructions, validate totals, reconcile sources, prepare exception lists, investigate suspected errors and produce owner-requested working papers or management reports.

The Operator relies on its legitimate interests in managing its accounts, preventing and detecting errors, and understanding business performance, and on legal obligations to maintain financial, tax and corporate records where those obligations apply. Intuit’s OAuth consent authorizes technical access; it is not treated as the sole data-protection basis for every item in the accounting records.

Control does not sell QuickBooks data and does not make decisions about people solely by automated means.

5. OpenAI processing boundary

If activated, data requested through the local connector would be provided to OpenAI Codex for the owner-requested analysis. The current account is an individual OpenAI account, not a Business or Enterprise workspace. OpenAI’s published guidance says content from services for individuals may be used to improve its models unless the relevant training controls are disabled.

The Operator will not transmit live QuickBooks data to Codex until the relevant account controls, written service-provider terms and international-transfer safeguards have been checked and documented as meeting Intuit’s and applicable Gibraltar legal requirements.

6. OAuth credentials, cookies and operational data

The intended public callback exchanges Intuit’s short-lived authorization code and encrypts the resulting refresh token for a private key held on the owner’s Mac. The application is designed not to persist the authorization code, access token, refresh token, realm identifier or QuickBooks report data in the hosted service.

When connection is enabled, a secure, HTTP-only state cookie lasts no more than ten minutes and is used only to protect the OAuth flow. The application does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Hosting and security systems may process IP address, timestamp, requested path, user agent, response status and other operational metadata. The application does not intentionally log OAuth query strings, authorization codes or tokens.

7. Providers and international transfers

  • Intuit provides QuickBooks Online and OAuth authorization.
  • Vercel hosts the public interface and callback; the callback function is configured to execute in London, United Kingdom, while provider control, delivery and security systems may operate elsewhere.
  • OpenAI would provide Codex analysis only after the pre-activation checks described above.

These providers may process data outside Gibraltar. Restricted transfers will not begin until the Operator has documented an applicable transfer basis and, where required, appropriate safeguards such as a Gibraltar International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum and a transfer risk assessment.

Data may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect legal rights and security. The Operator remains responsible for selecting, contracting with and monitoring its providers.

8. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth state expires within ten minutes. The hosted application does not intentionally retain authorization codes or tokens after the request completes.
  • Operator-controlled operational logs will be kept for no more than 30 days. Provider security records may be retained for the periods stated in the relevant provider terms.
  • The downloaded encrypted token package will be deleted within 24 hours after successful local import.
  • The local refresh token and company identifier will be deleted within 24 hours after disconnection or revocation, subject only to legal preservation duties.
  • Codex tasks containing QuickBooks data will be deleted when the requested review is complete and no later than 30 days afterward, unless a longer period is legally required and documented. OpenAI states that deleted account content is generally removed from its systems within a further 30 days, subject to stated legal and security exceptions.
  • Owner-exported reports and working papers retained as business records follow Barton Solutions Limited’s applicable accounting-record schedule and are reviewed for deletion when no longer required.

Disconnecting stops future API access but does not by itself delete an existing Codex task, exported report or working paper. Those items must be deleted separately. Assistance is available at info@era.food.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights to be informed, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Requests can be sent to the Operator using the contact details above. Individuals may also complain to the Gibraltar Regulatory Authority, as Information Commissioner.

10. Security and incidents

The intended production design uses HTTPS, OAuth state validation, owner-only connection controls, encrypted token handoff, restricted local credential files and application-level tool allowlists. No system can guarantee absolute security. Suspected unauthorized access should be reported immediately to info@era.food so access can be revoked and investigated.

11. Changes

This notice will be updated before any material expansion in data, purpose, providers or write capability. The effective date above identifies the current version.
