End-user licence agreement

Effective 5 August 2026

Control is not currently enabled to connect to live QuickBooks data. Activation remains subject to the safeguards and approvals described in this agreement.

1. Parties and scope

This agreement is between Barton Solutions Limited, of Unit R28, Ragged Staff Wharf, Gibraltar (the “Operator”), and the authorized owner or administrator using Control. Control is a private QuickBooks Online reporting connector and is not offered to the general public.

2. Limited licence

The Operator grants the authorized user a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable licence to use Control for internal accounting review and management reporting. The user must not share connection credentials, attempt to bypass access controls, resell the service or use it for an unauthorized QuickBooks company.

3. Authorization and company selection

The user must have authority to connect each QuickBooks company and must verify the exact company shown on Intuit’s consent screen. The user is responsible for revoking access promptly if that authority ends.

4. Read-only pilot boundary

During the initial pilot, Control’s local MCP server disables create, update and delete tools. Intuit’s accounting OAuth scope is broader than this application control. No accounting write should be enabled without a separate documented review and explicit owner approval.

5. Accounting responsibility

Control provides analysis and working information, not audit, tax, legal or professional accounting advice. Reports and classifications may be incomplete or incorrect. The user remains responsible for checking source records and obtaining appropriate professional approval before filings, payments, journal entries or statutory accounts.

6. Third-party services and data

Control depends on Intuit QuickBooks Online, Vercel and OpenAI Codex. The Operator remains responsible for selecting, contracting with and monitoring its providers. Use of Control is also subject to the published privacy notice and the applicable terms of those services.

The Operator must not activate live QuickBooks processing through an individual OpenAI account until the relevant data controls, written provider terms and Gibraltar international-transfer safeguards are documented as meeting Intuit’s and applicable legal requirements.

7. Security

The user must protect the local Mac, connection code, private key and Intuit account. Suspected loss or unauthorized access must be reported immediately so the QuickBooks authorization can be revoked. The user must not upload credentials into ordinary documents, messages or source control.

8. Suspension and termination

The Operator may suspend or terminate Control to protect security, comply with law or address a service failure. The user may stop using Control at any time by revoking the app in QuickBooks Online and deleting the local credentials. Sections that by their nature should survive termination will continue to apply.

9. Warranty and liability

Control is provided on an “as available” basis without a promise that it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Nothing in this agreement excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Subject to that, the Operator is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, or decisions made without checking the underlying QuickBooks records.
