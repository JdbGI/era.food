what is era lunch club?

your week, with lunch sorted.

ERA Lunch Club brings our restaurant food into your working day. It's for you as an individual: join with your own account, order for yourself and pay only for the lunches you choose. No subscription, no automatic charges — just your lunch.

Lunches are era. plates at our menu prices — see the full menu. Your Lunch Club account shows the options and prices available each week before you pay.

corporate catering & office lunches

the details

lunch club, answered.

What is ERA Lunch Club?

ERA Lunch Club is a weekly lunch service from era. in Gibraltar. Create a free account, choose the weekday lunches you want for the week ahead and pay online in one go. Lunch is then delivered to your workplace or ready to collect from the restaurant at Unit G24, ICC.

Is Lunch Club a subscription?

No. There is no subscription and there are no automatic charges. You pay only for the lunches you choose, when you confirm a week.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery anywhere in Gibraltar is £2.95 per lunch day. Collection from era. at Unit G24, ICC is free.

How do I join?

Register at lunch.era.food with your name and email address. Your personal ordering code appears on the next page and we email you a copy. Use the same code each week to open your Lunch Club account and choose your lunches.

What if I lose my personal code?

Your code can be replaced. Email info@era.food and we'll sort out a new one.

How do I pay?

You pay upfront through SumUp when you confirm your week. Nothing is charged automatically.

Which days can I order lunch for?

Lunch Club is for weekday lunches. Ordering opens each week for the week ahead: pick the days and meals you want, choose delivery or collection and confirm. Tick the reminder box when you register and we'll email you when next week's ordering opens.

What is on the Lunch Club menu?

era. plates at our menu prices: Mediterranean-inspired plates built around your choice of protein, sides and sauce, with meat, vegetarian and plant-based options. The options available each week, and their prices, are shown in your Lunch Club account before you pay. See the full menu for what we cook.

Can you cater for allergies and intolerances?

Yes. Allergen details for every item are on our live ordering menu. Tell us about any allergies or intolerances when you order, and if you have a severe allergy email info@era.food before ordering so we can talk it through.

My workplace already orders from era. Do I still need Lunch Club?

No. If your workplace has an era. lunch arrangement, use the private office link shared by your coordinator instead of a personal account. If you need the link, ask your coordinator.

Can I set up lunch for my whole team?

For team and office orders, use our corporate service instead. We set your company up on our ordering platform, delivery is free on 10 or more plates and every order gets a guaranteed slot. See corporate catering and office lunch delivery.
