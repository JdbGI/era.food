what's on the plate

a proper lunch, not a desk sandwich.

Every era. plate is built the same way: pick a protein, add two sides and finish with a house sauce. That structure does the nutrition work for you — protein to keep you full through the afternoon, fresh salad mix or sourdough alongside, and a house sauce to bring it together.

We cook with olive oil, keep the menu short and label allergens on every item. It's the same food we serve at Unit G24, ICC — just delivered to your office. See the full menu and prices.

the details

healthy office lunch, answered.

Do you deliver healthy lunches to offices in Gibraltar?

Yes. era. delivers Mediterranean plates to workplaces across Gibraltar. Every plate is built around a protein with two sides and a house sauce, cooked with olive oil. Delivery is free on orders of 10 plates or more, with a guaranteed delivery slot.

What are the high-protein options?

Every plate starts with a protein: chicken breast, chicken thigh, heart of rump steak, secreto ibérico, halloumi, falafel or crispy tofu. Chicken breast, steak and tofu are the leanest picks, and you can add a second protein to any plate.

Do you have vegan office lunch options?

Yes. Falafel and crispy tofu are vegan, as are the sourdough, salad mix, harissa, tahini and chimichurri, so a fully vegan plate is always available. Vegan items are marked (VE) on the menu.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Halloumi, falafel and crispy tofu are vegetarian, and every house sauce is vegetarian: lemon & herb yoghurt and hot honey & chilli are marked (V), and harissa, tahini and chimichurri are vegan too.

Can you cater for allergies and intolerances?

Allergens are labelled on every item on our live ordering menu. For example, the lemon & herb yoghurt contains milk and the tahini contains sesame. Tell us about any allergies or intolerances when you order. If someone on the team has a severe allergy, email info@era.food before ordering so we can talk it through.

Do you publish nutrition information?

Allergen and ingredient details are on the live ordering menu for every item. If your team needs more, such as macros for a training plan, email info@era.food and we'll help with what we can.

What oil do you cook with?

Olive oil. It's the base of the kitchen, the same way we cook in the restaurant at Unit G24, ICC.

Can each person choose their own plate?

Yes. On our ordering platform each person gets their own link and builds their own plate within a spending allocation you set, or one manager orders for everyone. Mixed diets, one order.

Is there a minimum order for office delivery?

Delivery is free on 10 plates or more. For smaller teams, email info@era.food and we'll see what we can do.
