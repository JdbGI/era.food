the details

office lunch, answered.

Do you offer corporate catering in Gibraltar?

Yes. era. currently runs a lunch delivery service for businesses in Gibraltar. Order Mediterranean plates for your team and we deliver them straight to your workplace.

Is office lunch delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders of 10 plates or more. For smaller team orders, email info@era.food and we'll see what we can do.

Is it a lot of management?

No. One manager can place orders for everyone, or each worker gets their own unique link and orders their own plate within a spending allocation you choose. Either way, there is nothing to chase and nothing to collect.

Can each worker choose their own food?

Yes. Every person builds their own plate — one protein, two sides and a house sauce — so nobody is stuck with someone else's choice. Vegetarian and vegan options are always available.

Can we see what the team is ordering and spending?

Yes. We send a detailed statement every week, so you can see exactly what your team ordered and what it cost — straightforward for budgets, expensing and keeping staff lunches under control.

Will lunch arrive on time?

Yes. Corporate orders come with a guaranteed delivery slot. We agree a time with you and your team's lunch arrives when we said it would.

Won't the team get bored of the same lunch?

No. Every plate is one protein, two sides and a house sauce, and the combinations rotate — so a standing office order can be different every day.

Can you cater for vegetarians, vegans and allergies?

Yes. There are vegetarian and vegan options on the menu, and each person builds their own plate. Tell us about any allergies when you place the order.

How do we set up a daily or weekly office lunch?

Email info@era.food with your company name, headcount, office address and preferred delivery time. We'll set it up as a one-off or a recurring team lunch.
